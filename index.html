Welcome to Lucky Sweets

Since 1985, Lucky Sweets has been the heart of Bhota's celebrations. What started as a small family mithai shop on Main Market Road has grown into the most trusted name for authentic Indian sweets across Hamirpur district and beyond.

Every sweet we make carries forward the rich tradition of Himachali and North Indian mithai craftsmanship. We use only pure desi ghee, fresh milk from local dairies, premium dry fruits and the finest ingredients — no shortcuts, no compromises.

  • Pure desi ghee — no vanaspati or vegetable oil
  • Fresh milk sourced daily from Hamirpur dairies
  • Premium quality dry fruits: almonds, cashews, pistachios
  • No artificial colours, flavours or preservatives
  • Handmade in small batches for guaranteed freshness
  • Specialised in wedding, festival and bulk celebration orders
