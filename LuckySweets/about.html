Our Story

From a humble family kitchen to Bhota's most loved sweet destination — this is our journey of passion, purity and taste.

A Family Legacy of Pure Mithai

In 1985, with nothing more than a brass karahi, a family recipe book and an unwavering commitment to quality, the founders of Lucky Sweets opened a small mithai counter on Main Market Road in Bhota. What began as a modest endeavour to serve the neighbourhood with freshly made Indian sweets has blossomed into the most trusted name in Hamirpur district and beyond.

For nearly four decades, every batch of our sweets has been prepared using pure desi ghee, fresh milk sourced daily from local dairies, premium dry fruits and the finest ingredients available. We have never used vanaspati, vegetable oil, artificial colours or preservatives — and we never will. Our experienced halwais (sweet makers) carry forward the art of traditional Indian confectionery, ensuring every piece of mithai that leaves our shop is a masterpiece of flavour and authenticity.

Today, Lucky Sweets is more than a shop — it is a part of Bhota's identity. From daily treats to grand wedding feasts, from festival celebrations to corporate gift hampers, generations of families have trusted us to sweeten their most cherished moments.

Our Mission & Vision

Guided by purpose and driven by a vision to bring the finest Indian sweets to every home.

Our Mission

To craft and deliver the most authentic, flavourful and hygienic Indian sweets using pure ingredients and time-honoured recipes — ensuring every customer experiences the true taste of tradition with every bite. We aim to make high-quality mithai accessible to everyone, from everyday treats to grand celebrations.

Our Vision

To become the most beloved and trusted sweet shop across Himachal Pradesh and beyond — recognised for uncompromising purity, exceptional taste and warm customer service. We envision expanding our reach while preserving the artisanal craft of traditional Indian sweet-making for future generations to cherish.

What Makes Us Special

Six pillars that define the Lucky Sweets experience and keep our customers coming back for more.

Pure Desi Ghee

Every sweet is prepared exclusively with pure desi ghee — never vanaspati, never vegetable oil. This commitment to authenticity gives our mithai its signature rich flavour and aroma that you can taste in every single bite.

Traditional Recipes

Our recipes have been lovingly preserved and perfected across generations. Each sweet follows authentic, time-tested methods passed down through our family — preserving the original flavours that have delighted customers for decades.

Fresh Daily

We prepare our sweets in small batches throughout the day to ensure maximum freshness. Nothing sits on the shelf for long — you will always get mithai that was made the same day, with the same care as if it were for our own family.

Hygienic Kitchen

Cleanliness and food safety are non-negotiable at Lucky Sweets. Our kitchen follows strict hygiene protocols, our staff wear gloves and hairnets, and we maintain spotless preparation areas — so you can enjoy your sweets with complete peace of mind.

Experienced Halwais

Our team of skilled halwais brings decades of combined expertise in the art of Indian sweet-making. Their trained hands and seasoned intuition ensure perfect texture, consistency and flavour in every single piece — something machines can simply never replicate.

Customer First

Your satisfaction is at the heart of everything we do. From helping you choose the right sweets for your occasion to accommodating custom orders and dietary preferences, we go the extra mile to ensure every visit to Lucky Sweets is a delightful experience.

Our Journey

Key milestones that shaped Lucky Sweets into the beloved brand it is today.

1985

The Beginning

Lucky Sweets was founded on Main Market Road, Bhota, with a simple dream — to serve the neighbourhood with pure, authentic Indian sweets made from traditional family recipes using only the finest ingredients.

1995

Menu Expansion

Responding to growing demand, we expanded our menu to include a wider variety of milk sweets, dry fruit delicacies and traditional Himachali specials — becoming a one-stop destination for all kinds of mithai.

2005

Bakery Division Launched

We added a full-fledged bakery section offering fresh cakes, pastries, cookies and bread — complementing our sweets range and giving customers even more reasons to visit Lucky Sweets every day.

2012

Bulk & Wedding Orders

Introduced dedicated bulk order services for weddings, corporate events, festivals and celebrations. Our customised sweet boxes and hampers quickly became the preferred choice across Hamirpur district.

2018

Shop Renovation & Modernisation

A complete renovation of our premises brought a modern, hygienic and customer-friendly shopping experience — while preserving the warm, traditional charm that our customers have always loved.

2024

Digital Presence & Online Ordering

Launched our website and online ordering capabilities, making it easier than ever for customers to browse our menu, place orders via WhatsApp and connect with us — bringing the Lucky Sweets experience to your fingertips.
