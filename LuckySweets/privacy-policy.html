Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to the Privacy Policy of Lucky Sweets. We are a premium Indian sweet shop located at Main Market Road, Bhota, Hamirpur, Himachal Pradesh — 176041, India. Since 1985, we have been serving the finest handmade mithai, bakery items and namkeen to families across Hamirpur district and beyond. Our tagline, "Fresh Sweets • Bakery • Namkeen," reflects our unwavering commitment to quality and freshness in everything we prepare.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, disclose and protect the personal information of visitors to our website (https://datyaltech-prog.github.io/luckysweets/) and customers who interact with us through our contact form, phone calls, WhatsApp messages or in-person visits to our shop. By accessing or using our website, you acknowledge that you have read, understood and agree to be bound by the terms of this Privacy Policy.

We take your privacy seriously and are committed to being transparent about our data practices. If you have any questions or concerns regarding this policy, please do not hesitate to contact us using the details provided in the Contact Us section below.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum amount of personal information necessary to provide you with our products, services and a positive browsing experience. The types of information we may collect include the following:

Personal Information Provided by You

  • Name: When you fill out the contact form on our website, we collect your full name so that we may address you properly and personalise our responses to your enquiries.
  • Phone Number: We collect your phone number solely for the purpose of responding to your enquiries, confirming orders and providing order updates via phone call or WhatsApp messaging.
  • Email Address: If you provide your email address through the contact form, we use it to reply to your messages and, if you have consented, to send you relevant updates about our products, seasonal offers or order confirmations.
  • Message Content: Any information you voluntarily share with us in the message body of the contact form, including order details, dietary preferences, delivery requirements or event-specific information related to weddings, festivals or other celebrations.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically by our server and through third-party analytics tools. This information is used solely to improve the performance and usability of our website and does not personally identify you unless you voluntarily provide such information through the contact form.

  • Device and Browser Information: The type of device, browser version and operating system you use to access our website.
  • IP Address: Your Internet Protocol (IP) address, which helps us understand the general geographic region of our visitors and detect potential security threats.
  • Pages Visited and Time Spent: We track which pages of our website you visit and the duration of your visit to help us understand which content is most useful and engaging for our audience.
  • Referring Source: The website or search engine that directed you to our site, which assists us in evaluating the effectiveness of our online marketing efforts.

What We Do Not Collect

We want to reassure you that we do not collect sensitive personal data such as financial information, Aadhaar numbers, PAN numbers, date of birth or government-issued identification numbers through our website. We do not use any third-party cookies or tracking technologies beyond Google Analytics for basic traffic analysis. Our website does not process online payments — all payments for orders are handled in person at our shop or through mutually agreed-upon methods.

3. How We Use Your Information

The personal information we collect from you is used solely for legitimate business purposes related to operating our sweet shop, communicating with you and enhancing the services we provide. Below are the specific purposes for which we use your information:

  1. Responding to Enquiries: When you submit a message through our contact form, we use your name, phone number and email address to respond to your questions, provide information about our sweets, discuss pricing and availability, and assist with special event orders such as weddings, festivals and corporate gifting.
  2. Processing Orders: If you place an order with us — whether for a single box of sweets or a bulk wedding order — we use the information you provide to prepare your order accurately, coordinate delivery timelines and ensure every detail meets your expectations.
  3. Sending Order Updates: We may contact you via WhatsApp or phone call to provide updates about your order status, confirm delivery details, notify you of the readiness of your sweets or communicate any changes related to your order.
  4. Improving Our Services: We use the aggregate data collected through Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website. This helps us identify popular products, improve page navigation, enhance loading speeds and tailor our content to better serve our customers.
  5. Customer Communication: With your explicit consent, we may periodically send you information about new sweet varieties, seasonal specials, festival offers or upcoming events at Lucky Sweets. You may opt out of such communications at any time by contacting us directly.
  6. Legal and Safety Compliance: In the rare event that we are required by applicable law, regulation or legal process to disclose your information, we will do so only to the extent necessary to comply with the law.

4. Information Sharing

We understand that your personal information is valuable and private. We want to be absolutely clear and transparent about our stance on sharing your data:

  • No Sale of Personal Data: We do not sell, rent, trade, lease or otherwise transfer your personal information to any third party for marketing, advertising or commercial purposes under any circumstances.
  • No Sharing with Third Parties: We do not share your personal information with third-party companies, data brokers, advertising networks or any other external organisations, except as described below or as required by law.
  • Service Providers: We may share limited, non-personally-identifiable aggregate data with service providers who assist us in operating our website, such as our web hosting provider and Google Analytics. These service providers are contractually obligated to use your data only for the specific purposes for which it was shared and must maintain appropriate security measures.
  • Legal Requirements: In the event that we receive a legally valid court order, subpoena, government request or are otherwise compelled by applicable Indian law to disclose your personal information, we will comply only after verifying the legitimacy of the request and only to the minimum extent required by law. We will notify you of such disclosure unless prohibited by law from doing so.
  • Business Transfers: In the unlikely event of a merger, acquisition, reorganisation or sale of all or a portion of our business assets, your personal information may be transferred as part of such transaction. The acquiring entity will be required to honour the commitments made in this Privacy Policy.

5. Data Security

Protecting the security of your personal information is a priority for Lucky Sweets. We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to safeguard your data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. While no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, we are committed to maintaining commercially acceptable standards of protection. Our specific security measures include:

  • SSL Encrypted Website: Our website is served over HTTPS using a valid SSL (Secure Sockets Layer) certificate. This means that all data transmitted between your browser and our server is encrypted, protecting it from interception by unauthorised third parties during transmission.
  • Secure Contact Form: The contact form on our website submits data securely, and any information you provide is stored in a protected environment with restricted access limited to authorised personnel only.
  • Limited Data Retention: We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected or as required by applicable law. Once your information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to securely delete or anonymise it.
  • Access Controls: Access to any personal information we store is restricted to authorised members of our team who require it to perform their duties. All personnel are expected to treat your data with confidentiality and are trained on basic data protection practices.
  • Secure Payment Processing: Since our website does not process online payments, no financial data such as credit card numbers, bank account details or UPI IDs are collected or stored on our servers. All payments are handled securely in person at our shop or through mutually agreed-upon offline methods.

Despite our best efforts, no system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure. If you become aware of any potential security vulnerability on our website or suspect that your information has been compromised, please contact us immediately at +91 98765 43210 so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

6. Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, tablet or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to the website owners. Below is an explanation of how we use cookies on the Lucky Sweets website:

Essential Cookies

Our website may place a small number of essential cookies that are strictly necessary for the website to function properly. These cookies do not collect personal information and cannot be disabled without affecting the core functionality of the site. They typically include session identifiers that help our server distinguish between different users and maintain a smooth browsing experience.

Google Analytics Cookies

We use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google Inc., to collect and analyse anonymised data about how visitors use our website. Google Analytics uses cookies to track information such as the number of visitors, the pages they visit, the average time spent on each page and the sources from which they arrived. This data is aggregated and does not personally identify individual users. We use this information solely to improve our website's performance, content and user experience.

Google Analytics collects data subject to its own Privacy Policy. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

How to Manage Cookies

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can set your browser to refuse all cookies, accept only certain cookies, or alert you when a cookie is being set. Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our website and your ability to use certain features. To manage your cookie preferences, consult the help documentation for your specific browser (Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Safari, Microsoft Edge, etc.).

10. Your Rights

We respect your rights regarding your personal information and are committed to helping you exercise those rights. While India currently does not have a single comprehensive data protection statute equivalent to the GDPR, we voluntarily adhere to internationally recognised principles of data protection and privacy. You have the following rights with respect to your personal data held by Lucky Sweets:

  1. Right to Access: You have the right to request information about the personal data we hold about you, including the specific categories of data collected, the purposes for which it is processed, the sources from which it was obtained and the third parties (if any) with whom it has been shared. Upon receiving a verified request, we will provide you with a copy of your personal data within a reasonable timeframe.
  2. Right to Correction: If you believe that any personal information we hold about you is inaccurate, incomplete or outdated, you have the right to request that we correct or update it. Please contact us with the specific details you wish to amend, and we will make the necessary corrections promptly.
  3. Right to Deletion: You have the right to request that we delete your personal information from our records. We will honour your request and securely delete your data, except where retention is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation, the resolution of a dispute, or the completion of a transaction that requires your data (such as an active order in progress).
  4. Right to Opt Out: If you have previously consented to receiving promotional communications from us via email, WhatsApp or phone, you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time. Simply contact us and let us know that you wish to stop receiving marketing messages, and we will remove you from our mailing or messaging lists promptly.
  5. Right to Lodge a Complaint: If you believe that your personal information has been mishandled, misused or that your privacy rights have been violated in any way, you have the right to raise a formal complaint with us. We take all complaints seriously and will investigate and respond to your concern within a reasonable period.

To exercise any of the rights listed above, please contact us using the information provided in the Contact Us section below. We may ask you to verify your identity before processing your request in order to protect your data from unauthorised access or disclosure.
