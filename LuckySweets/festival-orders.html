Navratri

During the nine sacred nights of Navratri, we prepare a special range of fast-friendly (vrat) sweets made with permitted ingredients — sabudana, rajgira, makhana and singhara. Every sweet is prepared in pure desi ghee, ensuring you can observe your fast without compromising on taste or tradition. Our Navratri thalis are available in bulk for community pandal celebrations.

Lohri

Celebrate the bonfire festival with our traditional winter special sweets. Our Gajak and Rewri are made using time-honoured recipes with jaggery, sesame seeds and peanuts — the classic Lohri combination. These crunchy, warming treats are perfect for sharing around the bonfire. Available in bulk packets for family, community and school Lohri celebrations.
