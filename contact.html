Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common questions about Lucky Sweets, our products and services.

What are your business hours?

We are open Monday to Saturday from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM and Sunday from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Our early morning fresh batch of sweets is ready by 7:00 AM every day. On festival days, hours may vary — we recommend calling us at +91 98765 43210 to confirm.

Do you take bulk orders for weddings?

Absolutely! We specialise in bulk orders for weddings, engagements, ring ceremonies and other celebrations. We offer customised sweet boxes, themed packaging and a wide variety of sweets to choose from. We recommend placing your order at least 3–5 days in advance for standard bulk orders and 7–10 days in advance for large wedding orders. Call us at +91 98765 43210 or message us on WhatsApp to discuss your requirements.

Do you deliver?

Currently, we offer local delivery within Bhota and nearby areas in Hamirpur district. For larger orders such as wedding or festival bulk orders, we can arrange delivery to Hamirpur town, Nadaun and surrounding areas. Delivery charges may apply depending on the distance and order size. For the freshest experience, we always recommend picking up your order directly from our shop. Please call us or WhatsApp us to enquire about delivery options for your specific location.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept multiple payment methods for your convenience: Cash, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), bank transfer/NEFT and credit/debit cards. For bulk and wedding orders, we also accept advance payments via bank transfer. Please speak to our staff at the counter for more details on payment options.

Can I customise my sweet box?

Yes! We offer fully customisable sweet boxes for all occasions. You can choose the sweets, quantities, packaging style and even add a personalised message or branding. Whether it’s a wedding favour box, a Diwali gift hamper, a corporate gift set or a birthday sweet box — we’ll create something special for you. Contact us via phone or WhatsApp to discuss your customisation needs and get a quote.

How fresh are your sweets?

Freshness is our top priority. We prepare sweets in small batches throughout the day to ensure every piece you buy is as fresh as possible. Our first batch is ready by 7:00 AM every morning. Milk-based sweets like Gulab Jamun, Rasgulla and Rasmalai are made fresh daily and are best consumed within 24–48 hours. Dry fruit sweets like Kaju Katli and Pista Roll have a longer shelf life of 5–7 days when stored properly. We never sell stale or day-old sweets — that’s the Lucky Sweets promise.
