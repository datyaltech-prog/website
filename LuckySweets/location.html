Getting Here

Multiple convenient ways to reach Lucky Sweets — by road, public transport or on foot.

By Road

From Hamirpur town, head towards Nadaun on the Hamirpur-Nadaun Road. Bhota is approximately 12 km from Hamirpur. Once you reach Bhota Main Bus Stand, Lucky Sweets is located on Main Market Road — look for our signboard opposite the State Bank of India branch. The drive takes about 20–25 minutes by car from Hamirpur town centre.

By Public Transport

Regular HPRTC and private buses run between Hamirpur Bus Stand and Bhota throughout the day. Alight at Bhota Main Bus Stand — Lucky Sweets is just a 2-minute walk from there. Buses to Nadaun, Kangra and Dehra also pass through Bhota. Ask for "Bhota Chowk" stop.

Walking Directions

From Bhota Main Bus Stand, walk towards the market area. Pass the State Bank of India on your right — Lucky Sweets is directly opposite. From Bhota Government Hospital, head towards the main road and turn right towards the market chowk. The shop is visible from the road with a prominent signboard.
